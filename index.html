Who we are

Hope begins with an opportunity to learn

Love for Hope is committed to helping children from less fortunate backgrounds stay in school, enrol with confidence and build a brighter future.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide scholarships and educational support to deserving students aged 9 to 18 years from less fortunate families, so that more young Kenyans can enrol in school, remain in school and prepare for a successful future.

🌍

Our Vision

A Kenya where every child, regardless of family background, has the opportunity to learn, grow and reach their full potential.

🤝

Our Approach

We verify legal documents, confirm school enrolment and conduct home assessments so that support reflects each family's actual level of need.

Our Objectives

  • Increase school enrolment and retention among disadvantaged children.
  • Provide scholarship support based on each student's real level of need.
  • Ensure support reaches genuine beneficiaries through verification and assessment.
  • Encourage hope, confidence and ambition.
  • Build partnerships with schools, communities and well-wishers.

Our Core Values

Compassion — We care deeply about every child we serve.

Integrity — We are honest and transparent in all we do.

Fairness — Support is based on need, without discrimination.

Accountability — We use resources responsibly and verify every beneficiary.

Hope — Every child deserves a future worth looking forward to.

Our process

Fair, careful and needs-based

Scholarship amounts are decided after verification and home assessment, not through a one-size-fits-all award.

01 · RegisterParent or guardian submits the student's application and registration fee.
02 · VerifyWe review birth certificate and proof of enrolment.
03 · ApproveEligible, verified students proceed to assessment.
04 · Home visitOur team understands the family's living conditions and level of need.
05 · ScholarshipSupport is determined based on assessment findings.

Registration fee

KSh 1,250 per student

The KSh 1,250 registration fee is used for verifying the student's legal documents and processing the registration. Payment is not a guarantee of scholarship award.

Eligibility stated in the official organisation profile: Kenyan citizens aged 9–18, from less fortunate backgrounds and enrolled in a registered school in Kenya.
