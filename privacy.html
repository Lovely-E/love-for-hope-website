Privacy & Child Protection Notice

Love for Hope collects student and guardian information only for application registration, eligibility verification, safeguarding, home assessment and scholarship administration.

Information collected

Information may include a student's name, date of birth, school and academic details; guardian contact and identity details; and copies of supporting documents submitted with consent.

How information is used

Information is accessed only by authorised personnel for the purposes explained above. Birth certificates, national identity numbers and private contact details must never be displayed publicly or sent as unsecured email attachments.

Security and retention

Applications and documents should be stored in access-controlled systems. Love for Hope should adopt a documented retention schedule, secure deletion process, incident response process and access review procedure.

Parent or guardian consent

A parent or legal guardian must provide informed consent before submitting a child's information. Contact the organisation to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and safeguarding obligations.
